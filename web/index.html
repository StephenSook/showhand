Showhand

Watch a simulated humanoid copy a recorded move, then see which seconds go back.

Each take is a phone recording beside a Unitree G1 in the MuJoCo simulator, driven through the same move by NVIDIA's GEAR-SONIC controller. Showhand measures the replay second by second, asks two vision models whether the poses match, and answers accept or names the seconds to show again.

Moves the recording and the robot to the start of those seconds.

No physical robot was used. The robot is NVIDIA's GEAR-SONIC whole-body controller driving a simulated Unitree G1 in MuJoCo, fed the body motion GEM-X estimated from the phone recording. Each pose here is MuJoCo's forward kinematics of the joint states the replay logged, sampled at the video's 30 frames per second.

Grades come from the committed take records in github.com/StephenSook/showhand. The limits are the frozen cutoffs in config/thresholds.yaml, set before the first graded take.

G1 meshes: Unitree Robotics, BSD 3-Clause, from NVIDIA GR00T-WholeBodyControl. See web/THIRD_PARTY.md.
